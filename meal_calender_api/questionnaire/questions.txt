# Meal Calendar — Questionnaire Questions
=====================================================

## Suggested Flow

Section 1: Goal          (1 question)
     ↓
Section 2: Diet          (2 questions)
     ↓
Section 3: Meals         (2 questions + dynamic sub-questions)
     ↓
Section 4: Kitchen       (3 questions)
     ↓
Section 5: Taste         (1 question)
     ↓
Section 6: Calories      (1 question)
     ↓
     ✅ Complete — Generate my meal plan!

## Section 3 — Your Meals
=====================================================

Field: meal_slots
Question: "Which meals do you want planned for you each day?"

Options (multi-select — user can pick any combination):
  - 🌅 Breakfast
  - ☀️  Lunch
  - 🌙 Dinner
  - 🍎 Snack

---


---

Field: unique_recipes_per_meal
Note: One sub-question per selected meal slot, shown dynamically after meal_slots is answered.

Example sub-questions (if user selected breakfast, lunch and dinner):
  - "How many different breakfast recipes do you want each week?"
  - "How many different lunch recipes do you want each week?"
  - "How many different dinner recipes do you want each week?"

Helper text shown under each:
  "1 = same meal every day, 3 = three different meals rotating across the week"

Input: Number selector — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

---
Field: meal_plan_repeat
Question: How often would you like to repeat a weekly meal plan
Options:
  - weekly
  - Every two weeks
  - Every 4 weeks

=====================================================
## Section 6 — Your Calories
=====================================================
Toggle that asks:
Question: "Do you care about counting calories?"
If yes then it can open the calory calculator if no will just set calories_per_day to the default of 2000 kcal

Field: calories_per_day
Question: "What is your daily calorie target?"

Input: Slider or number input — range 1200 to 3500 kcal

Helper text:
  "Not sure? A typical adult needs around 2000 kcal per day."

Optional:
  "Calculate for me" button — opens a simple BMR calculator using
  height, weight, age and activity level, and saves the result
  directly into this field.
